# LVS F1 Fantasy App

## An app for running F1 Fantasy Leagues for your friends!

## Features

### For Players

- Join F1 Fantasy Leagues
- Draft your team every race weekend to score points and compete with friends
- View race results and driver standings
- Compare your team's performance with friends
- Analyze your performance through detailed statistics and insights
- Use power ups to gain an advantage over your friends
- Use counterpicks to outsmart your opponents

### For League Managers

- Create and manage F1 Fantasy Leagues
- Add and remove players
- Track points and standings
- Manage user accounts and permissions
- Customize league settings and rules
- Manage player drafts
- Update race results and league points
- Import and export league data

### For Administrators

- Integrate with third-party services for additional functionality
- Provide real-time updates and notifications
- Support multiple languages and regional settings
- Implement advanced analytics and reporting
- Ensure data security and privacy
